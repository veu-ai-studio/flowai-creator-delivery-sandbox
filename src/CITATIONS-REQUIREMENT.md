# FlowAI Citations & Sources Requirement

**Effective:** Sprints 7–10 and all future sprints  
**Priority:** Non-negotiable quality & credibility standard  
**Scope:** Every LLM-generated output with statistics, benchmarks, or data points

---

## Requirement Overview

Every statistic, percentage, benchmark, market size figure, data point, or quantified claim generated by any LLM call anywhere in FlowAI **must include a real, verifiable source citation** in parentheses immediately after the figure.

### Why This Matters

Unsourced statistics in investor materials, pilot proposals, and executive summaries create:
- **Legal risk** — C-suite buyers fact-check claims; fabricated data destroys credibility
- **Reputational damage** — "Benchmarking" without sources raises red flags
- **Deal risk** — VCs and strategic partners will reject decks with unverified claims

A pitch deck with properly cited statistics from EPA, AASHE, and WHO is fundable. A pitch deck with uncited statistics is a liability.

---

## Format Specification

### Correct Format (with source)

```
87% of sustainability directors cite data silos as a major barrier
(Source: AASHE Sustainability Tracking Survey, 2023)
```

```
The global sustainability market is valued at $12.6 billion annually
(Source: McKinsey & Company, 2024)
```

```
77% of enterprise CIOs prioritize cloud infrastructure (Source: Gartner CIO Agenda Study, 2023)
```

### Incorrect Format (without source)

❌ "87% of sustainability directors cite data silos as a major barrier"  
❌ "The global market is valued at $12.6 billion"  
❌ "Industry research shows that 77% of CIOs…"

---

## Acceptable Source Types

✅ Government agencies (EPA, NOAA, CDC, USDA, UNESCO)  
✅ Industry associations (AASHE, APPA, EGI, ACE)  
✅ Major research firms (McKinsey, Gartner, IDC, Nielsen, Forrester)  
✅ International organizations (WHO, World Bank, UN)  
✅ Peer-reviewed publications (with year & author)  
✅ Academic institutions (with study name & year)  
✅ Industry-specific databases (if citable)

❌ Vague generalities ("industry research shows…")  
❌ Unnamed studies  
❌ AI-invented statistics  
❌ Outdated sources (>5 years old without justification)

## Implementation: Updated LLM Prompts

Every affected prompt has been updated to include this instruction:

```
CRITICAL — CITATIONS FOR ALL DATA: 
For every statistic, percentage, benchmark, market size figure, or data point you include, 
provide a source citation in parentheses immediately after the figure. 
Use real, verifiable sources — EPA, AASHE, WHO, McKinsey, Gartner, IDC, Nielsen, industry associations, 
government databases, or peer-reviewed research. 
Format as: [statistic] (Source: [Organization], [Year]). 
Never invent statistics. If you cannot cite a real source for a figure, do not include that figure.
```

## Compliance Verification

To verify compliance before publishing any investor materials, GTM assets, or demo content:

1. **Read every statistic** in the generated output
2. **Check for citations** in parentheses immediately after each figure
3. **Verify source credibility** — Is it from EPA, Gartner, a peer-reviewed study, etc.?
4. **Spot-check sources** — Do a quick Google search to confirm the source exists and is reputable
5. **Flag and reject** any uncited claims, vague generalizations, or invented-sounding statistics
