## All distinct objections (37)
**01. [Slot 1] Telemetry-only dims create shadow metrics**

> §10.1 dims 6-7 emit telemetry that operators will inevitably use for decisions despite being excluded from §7.6 score. This creates a shadow metric system where content_quality_finding and accessibility_finding accumulate in dashboards but have no canonical interpretation or threshold.

**02. [Slot 1] Purpose field atomicity gap**

> CA-15-B-v2 adds product_purpose as optional free-form text on product_registry but provides no atomic-write guarantee. Unlike the 6 canonical blocks protected by CA-14-D-Q1, this field could be partially written or corrupted without triggering rollback.

**03. [Slot 1] Drift tier thresholds undefined**

> CA-15-C-v2-Q3 mentions 20%/50% token-change thresholds in options but the proposal text never defines how PURPOSE_DRIFT_MINOR/MAJOR/CRITICAL are calculated. Without concrete thresholds, operators cannot predict when drift notifications will trigger.

**04. [Slot 1] CONFORMANCE_SCOPE.md creates circular dependency**

> CA-15-D-v2 delegates CEO re-sign triggers to docs/governance/CONFORMANCE_SCOPE.md which doesn't exist yet. This file must list which files trigger re-sign, but editing CONFORMANCE_SCOPE.md itself could require re-sign, creating an infinite regress.

**05. [Slot 2] Admin-only opt-in complexity**

> The proposal introduces an admin-only opt-in mechanism for dimensions 6 and 7 in §10.1, which could complicate the configuration process and limit flexibility for operators who may need these dimensions without admin intervention.

**06. [Slot 2] Telemetry-only findings risk**

> By making content_quality and accessibility findings telemetry-only, the proposal risks underemphasizing these important aspects, potentially allowing products with poor content quality or accessibility to pass audits without sufficient scrutiny.

**07. [Slot 2] Purpose information ambiguity**

> The removal of the `purpose_record` block and reliance on an optional free-form `product_purpose` field could lead to inconsistencies in how purpose information is captured and used, as there is no structured schema or validation.

**08. [Slot 3] Lack of Validation**

> The proposal does not provide sufficient validation for the `product_purpose` field, which could lead to inconsistent or misleading data. Specifically, §7.5 does not define any validation rules for this field.

**09. [Slot 3] Inconsistent Scoring**

> The §7.6 score formula does not account for content quality and accessibility findings, which could result in an incomplete or inaccurate assessment of the product's quality. This is evident in the §7.6 score formula, which only considers crit, high, med, and low findings.

**10. [Slot 3] Overreliance on Admin Opt-in**

> The proposal relies heavily on admin opt-in for dims 6 and 7, which may not be sufficient to ensure that these dimensions are properly considered. This is a concern because the admin opt-in mechanism is not clearly defined in §10.1.

**11. [Slot 4] Complexity of admin-opt-in mechanism**

> The admin-opt-in mechanism for extending the quality dimensions in §10.1 introduces unnecessary complexity. It requires admin intervention for enabling dimensions that could be beneficial for all products. This might lead to inconsistent quality audits across different products.

**12. [Slot 4] Lack of LLM-judged purpose-alignment check**

> Removing the LLM-judged purpose-alignment check from the Purpose-Driven Optimization loop exit criteria may result in optimizations that do not align with the product's purpose. This could lead to suboptimal outcomes that do not meet the intended goals of the product.

**13. [Slot 4] Potential misuse of free-form product_purpose field**

> The optional free-form `product_purpose` field in the ProductSSOT may lead to inconsistent and unvalidated purpose statements. Without proper validation, this field could be misused or left empty, defeating the purpose of having a product_purpose field in the first place.

**14. [Slot 4] Risk of false positives in drift annotation**

> The tiered drift annotation system may lead to false positives, especially in the major and critical tiers. This could result in unnecessary operator interventions and potential freezes on fixes, disrupting the continuous improvement process.

**15. [Slot 5] Admin-opt-in complexity**

> The admin-opt-in mechanism for dimensions 6 and 7 introduces unnecessary complexity and potential for misconfiguration.

**16. [Slot 5] Telemetry-only findings**

> Content quality and accessibility findings being telemetry-only may lead to these issues being overlooked by operators.

**17. [Slot 5] Optional purpose field**

> The optional nature of the `product_purpose` field may result in inconsistent or missing purpose statements across products.

**18. [Slot 6] Admin-only opt-in complexity**

> The admin-only opt-in for dims 6 & 7 in §10.1 adds complexity without a clear benefit. It introduces a configuration flag that needs to be managed and understood, potentially leading to confusion and inconsistent application of quality checks across products.

**19. [Slot 6] Telemetry-only findings risk neglect**

> Treating content_quality and accessibility findings as telemetry-only in §7.6 risks neglecting these important aspects of product quality. If these findings don't directly impact the score, they may be deprioritized or ignored, leading to a decline in overall product quality.

**20. [Slot 6] Optional product_purpose ambiguity**

> The optional free-form `product_purpose` field in `product_registry` lacks structure and validation, making it difficult to use effectively. The absence of a schema and the reliance on operator-supplied text can lead to inconsistent and unreliable purpose statements.

**21. [Slot 6] Tiered drift annotation complexity**

> The tiered drift annotation (minor/major/critical) adds unnecessary complexity to the system. The distinctions between the tiers and their corresponding actions may not be clear, leading to inconsistent application and potential confusion for operators.

**22. [Slot 6] Advisory SSOT-Conformance Gate weakness**

> Making the §27 SSOT-Conformance Gate advisory-only weakens its effectiveness. Without a hard blocking gate, operators may choose to ignore conformance issues, leading to a decline in the overall quality and reliability of the system.

**23. [Slot 7] §10.1 dimension overlap**

> The proposed Content Quality dimension overlaps with existing UI/UX scoring criteria without clear differentiation boundaries. This creates ambiguity in §10.1 audit reports where the same visual/text issues could be counted under both dimensions, leading to inconsistent weighting and potential double-reporting.

**24. [Slot 7] Optional purpose field risk**

> The free-form product_purpose field lacks validation or structured schema, making it vulnerable to misuse and misinterpretation. Without mandatory format rules or automated consistency checks, operators could enter contradictory, outdated, or overly vague purpose statements that undermine drift detection mechanisms.

**25. [Slot 7] Drift thresholds arbitrary**

> The 20%/50% token-change thresholds for minor/major/critical drift annotations lack empirical justification. These arbitrary cutoffs may misclassify significant semantic changes as minor if token delta is low, or flag harmless refactors as critical drift, creating noise in governance records.

**26. [Slot 7] Conformance scope maintenance**

> Maintaining CONFORMANCE_SCOPE.md as the trigger for CEO re-sign introduces documentation drift risk. The file requires manual updates for every relevant CA, creating a brittle dependency where missed updates could invalidate sign-offs or allow non-conforming changes to bypass scrutiny.

**27. [Slot 8] Telemetry-only weakens enforcement**

> CA-15-A-v2-Q2 moves content_quality and accessibility findings out of §7.6 entirely and into dashboard-only telemetry. That means a product can repeatedly fail copy/accessibility checks while still posting a clean readiness score, which undermines the existing score as an operator-facing risk signal.

**28. [Slot 8] Admin-opt-in hides regressions**

> In CA-15-A-v2-Q3, dims 6 and 7 are off by default and admin-only, but the proposal still exposes them as quality dimensions in §10.1. This creates a split-brain audit where most products never see these checks in the acceptance path, so regressions can ship unless someone remembers to enable the knob product-by-product.

**29. [Slot 8] Purpose field is too underspecified**

> CA-15-B-v2 replaces the canonical `purpose_record` with an optional free-form `product_purpose` string on `product_registry`, but provides no schema, length limit, provenance, or write-atomicity rule beyond a general note. That invites stale, contradictory, or promotional purpose text to become de facto canonical state without any structured validation or lifecycle management.

**30. [Slot 8] Drift annotations may mask deadlocks**

> CA-15-C-v2-Q3 converts PURPOSE_DRIFT into minor/major/critical annotations, but the proposal does not specify a deterministic transition rule from annotation to operator disposition or freeze. In practice, major drift can accumulate across runs while still allowing the loop to continue, making the issue visible but not actionable.

**31. [Slot 8] Scoped re-sign is gameable**

> CA-15-D-v2-Q2 limits CEO re-sign invalidation to files listed in `docs/governance/CONFORMANCE_SCOPE.md`. Because that scope file is itself editable documentation, the system can drift into a state where consequential governance changes are made adjacent to, but not inside, the tracked scope and therefore avoid re-sign requirements.

**32. [Slot 9] Overly Complex §10.1 Extension**

> The proposal extends §10.1 with two new dimensions (Content Quality and Accessibility) but introduces complexity with admin-opt-in and telemetry-only findings. This may lead to inconsistent application and confusion among operators.

**33. [Slot 9] Lack of Validation for `product_purpose`**

> The optional `product_purpose` field is introduced without a strict schema or validation rules, potentially leading to inconsistent or malformed data entry.

**34. [Slot 9] Removal of `purpose_record` Block**

> Removing `purpose_record` as a canonical block may reduce the system's ability to track and validate product purpose, potentially impacting Purpose-Driven Optimization.

**35. [Slot 10] §10.1 dim extension overlap unresolved**

> CA-15-A v2 extends the existing §10.1 5-dim engine (UI/UX, API, Logic, Business Value, Security Posture) with optional dims 6/7 but provides no explicit conflict-resolution rule for UI/UX vs Content Quality double-counting, despite citing Slot 1 and Slot 10 objections from v1.

**36. [Slot 10] product_purpose field lacks validation**

> CA-15-B v2 adds optional free-form product_purpose to product_registry without required-field rules, atomic-write enforcement, or schema validation, directly contradicting the §7.5 atomic ProductSSOT update invariant and CA-14-D-Q1 guarantees.

**37. [Slot 10] §27 gate advisory weakens §25 rules**

> CA-15-D v2 converts the §27 SSOT-Conformance Gate to advisory-only telemetry via governance_record_entry, eroding the Locked Rules §25 requirement that every CA promotion must maintain strict conformance without operator bypass.
